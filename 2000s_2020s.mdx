# 2000s | Online Gaming & Sixth Generation

## Era Description

Gaming became more connected during the 2000s. Faster processors and dedicated graphics chips made detailed 3D worlds common on both PCs and consoles. Broadband internet allowed online multiplayer to grow beyond small communities. DVDs also gave developers more storage, so games could include recorded dialogue, larger maps, and longer cutscenes.

## Popular Games

- **Halo 2 (2004)**  
  One of the first console games to make online multiplayer a major feature through Xbox Live.

- **Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas (2004)**  
  Its large open world and player freedom showed how much larger games had become.

- **Half-Life 2 (2004)**  
  Known for realistic physics through the Source Engine and its use of facial animation.

- **World of Warcraft (2004)**  
  Expanded online role-playing games by supporting thousands of players on shared servers.

- **Shadow of the Colossus (2005)**  
  Used large environments and minimal storytelling to show a different approach to game design.

## Popular Gaming Devices

- **PlayStation 2**  
  The best-selling console of all time with more than 160 million units sold.

- **Xbox**  
  Introduced Xbox Live, making online console gaming easier to access.

- **Nintendo GameCube**  
  Focused on performance and exclusive first-party games despite lower sales.

- **PlayStation Portable**  
  Brought console-style graphics to a handheld system.

- **Nintendo DS**  
  Its dual screens and touch controls changed how portable games were designed.

## Key Improvements

Hardware shifted toward stronger graphics processors instead of relying mostly on the CPU. Broadband internet became common enough for online multiplayer to become a standard feature. DVD storage increased game size from about 700 MB on CDs to 4.7 GB on single-layer DVDs. Physics engines also became more reliable, allowing objects and characters to react more naturally.

# 2010s | Mobile, Indie, & Streaming

## Era Description

Gaming reached more people during the 2010s. Smartphones became capable gaming devices, while digital stores made publishing easier for independent developers. Streaming platforms turned gameplay into entertainment, and esports grew into organized competitions watched by millions.

## Popular Games

- **Minecraft (2011)**  
  Its block-based world encouraged creativity instead of fixed objectives.

- **The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt (2015)**  
  Raised expectations for open-world storytelling and side quests.

- **Overwatch (2016)**  
  Popularized hero-based multiplayer shooters with distinct character abilities.

- **Fortnite (2017)**  
  Combined battle royale gameplay with live events and cross-platform support.

- **The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild (2017)**  
  Rewarded exploration through open-ended mechanics instead of linear progression.

## Popular Gaming Devices

- **PlayStation 4**  
  Focused on digital services and stronger graphics hardware.

- **Xbox One**  
  Expanded online services through Xbox Game Pass and cloud features.

- **Nintendo Switch**  
  Combined handheld and home console gaming in one system.

- **Smartphones**  
  Made gaming available without dedicated gaming hardware.

- **Oculus Rift**  
  Helped bring consumer virtual reality back into the market.

## Key Improvements

Digital downloads became as common as physical copies. Graphics improved through physically based rendering, which handled lighting and materials more accurately. Mobile processors became powerful enough for larger games. Cloud storage kept saved progress online, and streaming platforms changed how people discovered and followed games.

# 2020s | Next Generation & Modern Frontiers

## Era Description

Current hardware focuses on speed and image quality. Solid-state storage reduced loading times, while ray tracing improved lighting and reflections. Artificial intelligence also started assisting parts of game development. Cloud gaming and cross-platform play continue to reduce the limits between different gaming systems.

## Popular Games

- **Cyberpunk 2077 (2020)**  
  Pushed modern hardware with detailed environments and advanced graphics.

- **Elden Ring (2022)**  
  Built a large open world with few restrictions on exploration.

- **Baldur's Gate 3 (2023)**  
  Used complex dialogue systems and player choice on a much larger scale.

- **Black Myth: Wukong (2024)**  
  Showcased modern graphics technologies using Unreal Engine 5.

- **Marvel Rivals (2024)**  
  Combined hero-based multiplayer gameplay with destructible environments.

## Popular Gaming Devices

- **PlayStation 5**  
  Uses a custom NVMe SSD to reduce loading times.

- **Xbox Series X**  
  Supports high frame rates, ray tracing, and fast storage.

- **Nintendo Switch OLED**  
  Improved the display while keeping the original hardware design.

- **Steam Deck**  
  Runs many PC games in a portable form.

- **Meta Quest 3**  
  Supports standalone virtual reality without requiring a gaming PC.

## Key Improvements

High-speed NVMe SSDs changed how games load data, making larger worlds possible without long loading screens. Hardware ray tracing improved reflections, shadows, and lighting. AI tools started helping developers create animations, voices, and game assets. Cross-platform multiplayer became more common, allowing players on different systems to play together more easily.
